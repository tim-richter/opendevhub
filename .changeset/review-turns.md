Each session has its own page: click a session's title in a checkout. It shows the session's cost, tokens by kind, how full its context window is, its subagents, and its turns, newest first, each with its prompt, duration, cost, tokens, tool calls and the agent's last reply. Expand a turn to see what it changed and comment on the lines; the comments go to that session. A box on the page sends the session another prompt.
